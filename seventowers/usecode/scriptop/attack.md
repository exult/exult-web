```usecode
attack
```

Used in conjunction with '`set_to_attack`' intrinsic to synchronize animations. After a call to '`set_to_attack`' intrinsic is made, the '`attack`' command causes the readied attack to happen. It was only used with missile attacks in the originals (e.g., ranged spells), but you could also attack with a melee weapon if the target was within range.
