### <a id="directives"></a>Compiler directives

Compiler directives are special commands which alter the way that the Usecode C Compiler parses the source code file. All compiler directives must stand on a line of their own; although they do not need to be ended with a semi-colon, you must ensure that there is a new-line character at their end (i.e., be sure to press 'Enter' at the end of the line).
